Indiamart Lead Notifier

Never miss a buy lead on IndiaMART again.

Indiamart Lead Notifier is a Chrome extension for IndiaMART sellers. It watches your seller dashboard for new buy leads matching filters you set (price, quantity, lead age, state, city, keywords), automatically purchases matching leads on your behalf, and instantly alerts your phone with the buyer's contact details — so you can call back before a competitor does.

What it does

  • Continuously monitors your IndiaMART seller account for new buy leads
  • Filters leads by price, quantity, lead age, location, and keywords
  • Automatically purchases matching leads using your IndiaMART balance
  • Sends an instant phone notification with the buyer's name, mobile number, city, and product details
  • Optionally logs every purchased lead as a row in a Google Sheet you choose
